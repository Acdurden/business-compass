What gets built, in order

  1. A "test" switch on each assessment. Admin-only. Tests stay in the app but drop out of every metric. Needed first, or the pilot numbers start polluted.
  2. A place to keep market figures. One new list in the database: which figure, which period, the value, the link, who entered it and when. Rows are only ever added.
  3. The "Add figures" form on the Market figures tab, admin-only, with the eight figures above set up and today's history already loaded.
  4. The Insights page with the Our assessments and Market figures tabs, advisor and admin only.
  5. The market card on each client's advisor page. Reads the latest market figures at the moment it is opened.

Nothing here changes a score, a section total, a multiple or anything a client sees. The assessment metrics are read from data the app already stores.

Decisions for you

  1. Which assessments are tests. Today all nine; the first real pilot client is the first one that counts.
  2. Who can add market figures: admins only, or any advisor.
  3. Whether the grade column appears before the rating scale is approved, or waits for that decision.
  4. Whether the client-file card should also flag when a client's valuation sits below the whole market range for their size. Today it would flag 5 of the 6 tests that entered EBITDA, because the model's multiples mostly run below the published agency range for the same size.
